The team

About the Founders

Experienced professionals united by a shared passion for unlocking human potential and fostering personal and professional growth.

Dr. Ruth Muthei (PhD)
Founder & Lead Coach

An experienced educationist with over 30 years of experience, passionate about unlocking human potential towards the realization of her clients' purpose. As a Personal & Professional Development coach, Ruth focuses on helping clients maximize their skills, talents, and interests to find fulfillment in their career paths.

Dr. Ruth holds certifications as a Certified Organizational Effectiveness Coach (COEC) from CDI-AFRICA and a Certified Psychometric Assessment Administrator (UK Podium 365). She specializes in career coaching for college-bound students, youth coaching, life skills and career planning, personal and professional development, organizational effectiveness coaching, as well as pre-retirement and retirement coaching. She also serves as an Education Consultant & Trainer.

COEC Certified Psychometric Assessor Career Coaching Youth Coaching Education Consultant 30+ Years Experience
Pastor Isabella Mwikali
Co-Founder & Counselling Lead

Pastor Isabella Mwikali holds a Bachelor's degree in Education and has a great wealth of experience in education and school administration. She is a Certified Psychometric Assessment Administrator (UK Podium365) and is dedicated to empowering individuals and organizations through individual/group counselling and training programs.

She is a children's minister to the core and also focuses on mentoring teens and youth, providing training for Sunday school teachers, and conducting parenting programs. With a diploma in theology and a certificate in counseling psychology, Isabella brings a holistic approach to her counselling and mentoring services.
