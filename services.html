Journey with Dr. Ruth

A full one-year mentorship program led personally by Dr. Ruth Muthei, tailored for young adults (18–24) and young professionals (24–35). Comprehensive support in all areas of life — to help you navigate this critical season with confidence, clarity and purpose.

Start Your Journey
🧭
🌟
Personal Development

Explore your strengths, values, and passions. Foster deep self-awareness and a clear sense of purpose to anchor every decision you make.

💼
Career Planning & Development

Identify career aspirations, set strategic goals, build a powerful resume, and master interview techniques to land the right role.

🏆
Leadership & Professional Skills

Develop essential leadership qualities, professional communication, and the mindset needed for success in any field or industry.

📣
Personal Branding & Networking

Build your personal brand, leverage networking opportunities, and use social media strategically to grow your professional presence.

💰
Financial Literacy

Financial management, budgeting, savings strategies, and making informed decisions about money — skills that transform your future.

⚖️
Work-Life Balance

The importance of self-care, boundaries, and balance. Learn to maintain your well-being while pursuing ambitious professional goals.

🧠
Emotional Intelligence

Develop emotional intelligence, effective communication, and relationship-building skills that will serve you in every area of life.

🌿
Mental Health & Wellness

Stress management, resilience-building, and maintaining strong mental well-being throughout life's challenges and transitions.

🔁
Life Transitions & Decision Making

Navigate key life transitions with confidence. Dr. Ruth walks with you through every major decision and change in this pivotal season.

How the Journey with Dr. Ruth Works
  • Duration: Full 12-month engagement with monthly one-on-one sessions with Dr. Ruth
  • Format: In-person or virtual sessions available (Nairobi-based participants preferred in-person)
  • Assessment: Psychometric assessment at the start to map your strengths and areas for growth
  • Goal Setting: A personal development roadmap created collaboratively with Dr. Ruth
  • Resources: Access to workbooks, exercises, and recommended reading throughout the year
  • Community: Access to a peer community of fellow program participants
  • Accountability: Regular check-ins and milestone reviews to keep you on track
  • Graduation: End-of-year celebration and portfolio of your transformation journey
